# Privacy policy

*Last updated November 19, 2026*

I-5 Express doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## What the app does on the network

The app downloads [WSDOT's express lanes page](https://wsdot.wa.gov/travel/roads-bridges/express-lanes) straight from the Washington State Department of Transportation. It contacts no other server. Like any web request, this tells WSDOT your IP address and that the request came from this app. WSDOT's own [privacy notice](https://wsdot.wa.gov/about/policies/web-privacy-notice) covers what it does with that.

Links in the app, such as the WSDOT travel map or @wsdot_traffic, open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## What stays on your phone

- The last copy of WSDOT's page, so the widget works offline.
- Your settings, such as how often to check and whether to send notifications.
- A note of the last status it saw, so it can tell when the lanes change.

None of this leaves your phone. The app turns off Android's cloud backup for its data. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** to download WSDOT's page.
- **Notifications:** only if you turn on alerts. Alerts are created on your phone.
- **Alarms and reminders:** to update the widget at the minute the lanes change.
- **Run at startup:** to set those alarms again after the phone restarts.

## Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
